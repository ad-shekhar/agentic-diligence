Ten Specialized Diligence Engines

Every module is backed by deterministic mathematical formulas, explicit epistemic bounds, and reproducible evidence references.

1. Performance & Autonomy Intelligence

Runtime Execution

Autonomy & Human Intervention Rate

Measures the exact percentage of workflows completed 100% autonomously vs. those requiring human staff assistance. Classifies interventions into explicit human telemetry spans and inferred reaction-latency anomalies.

AR = Lower Bounded [lower, upper]

Telemetry-Attributable Unit Economics

Computes the true Cost Per Task (CPT) down to fractions of a cent ($0.0001 USD) by pricing observed prompt and completion tokens against vendor rate cards, isolating model fees from infrastructure overhead.

CPT = Sum(Model Cost) / Total Tasks

Token Efficiency & Latency Drift

Evaluates token utilization ratios (output vs. prompt context ratio) and monitors execution latency degradation across multi-turn reasoning loops.

TE = output_tokens / (input + output)

2. Architecture & Supply Chain Intelligence

Software Supply Chain

AI Bill of Materials (AIBOM)

An itemized supply chain inventory cataloging all foundation models, embedding endpoints, vector databases, external APIs, and human review gates. Exports natively to the official OWASP CycloneDX 1.6 standard.

Standards: CycloneDX v1.6 & v1.5 JSON

Single Points of Failure (SPOF) & Cascades

Identifies fragile dependencies where a single API timeout causes runaway retry loops, measuring the cost overrun multiplier (e.g. 1.8x–5.4x cost spike on failure).

Cascade Multiplier = Failed Cost / OK Cost

Vendor Concentration (HHI Index)

Calculates the Herfindahl-Hirschman Index across model and cloud providers to detect single-vendor lock-in risks (e.g. 98% reliance on OpenAI).

HHI = Sum(Share^2) * 10,000

3. Trust, Security & Verification

Evidence & Governance

Claim Verification Matrix

Pits stated claims against measured runtime evidence. Emits formal verdicts: Verified, Contradicted, Partially Verified, or Insufficient Evidence, with explicit mathematical explanations.

Epistemic Labels: [OBSERVED], [CALCULATED]

Tool Execution Risk & Blast Radius

Classifies tool invocations into Read-Only, Write/Modify, and Dangerous OS Execution. Audits whether the AI has unconstrained access to production databases or bash terminals without human approval.

Risk Score: 0 (Safe) to 100 (Critical Hazard)

Proof Graph & Cryptographic Seals

Every claim verdict links directly to the supporting trace IDs, span records, and SHA-256 payload provenance digests. Packages are sealed into tamper-proof manifest.json bundles.

Chain of Custody: Pre-Parsing SHA-256

4. Investment & Strategic Diligence

Deal Execution

Defensibility & Moat Analysis

Evaluates whether the agent possesses proprietary scaffolding, model weight sovereignty, and domain data flywheels, or is merely a thin API wrapper that can be replicated in 2 weeks.

Outputs: Est. Months & USD to Replicate

What-If Economic Stress Simulator

Interactive simulator projecting gross margin impacts under 5x–10x volume surges, +25% provider token price shocks, and human operator wage adjustments ($28/hr vs $55/hr).

Simulates: Fully-Loaded Gross Margins

Investment Committee (IC) Deal Memo

Synthesizes technical findings into an executive memo featuring a definitive Buy, Pass, or Conditional recommendation, Red/Yellow/Green flag register, and 100-day engineering action plan.

Exports: PDF, JSON, ZIP Diligence Bundle
