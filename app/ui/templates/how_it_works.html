METHODOLOGY & PIPELINE

From Raw Telemetry to Verifiable Investment Verdicts

A 4-stage pipeline that eliminates subjective speculation through empirical software flight recorder logs, deterministic mathematics, and cryptographic non-repudiation.

1

Stage 1: Connect & Ingest Telemetry

The target agent system streams execution traces into Agentic Diligence via standard OpenTelemetry GenAI protocols or out-of-process proxies.

Pre-Parsing Provenance Guarantee

Before JSON deserialization occurs, the exact incoming byte stream is digested with SHA-256. This fingerprint is stored in payload_provenance, ensuring an immutable chain of custody that cannot be repudiated.

POST /v1/traces -> SHA-256(raw_bytes) -> Store PayloadProvenance
2

Stage 2: Normalize & Segment Tasks

Raw spans from LangChain, CrewAI, AutoGen, or custom agents are normalized to OpenTelemetry GenAI v1.28.0 standards.

Task Gap Analysis & Reaction Latency Heuristic

Spans are clustered into atomic tasks. Inter-span latencies exceeding the threshold (e.g. >30s) are analyzed to distinguish autonomous computing delays from human intervention pauses. Gaps without telemetry are categorized as [UNKNOWN], preventing missing data from being counted as success.

NormalizedSpan -> segment_trace() -> compute_diligence_metrics()
3

Stage 3: Mathematically Verify Claims

Stated claims from founder pitch decks, investor memos, and vendor SLAs are translated into formal schemas (operator, target threshold, unit).

Bounded Epistemic Evaluation

The claim verifier computes statistical lower and upper confidence bounds. If an observed metric falls short of the target direction or has insufficient telemetry sample size (n < 15), it emits a CONTRADICTED or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE verdict with full mathematical explanations.

ClaimSchema vs. EpistemicValue[lower, point, upper] -> Verdict
4

Stage 4: Decide & Compile Evidence Package

Compiles all findings into board-ready deliverables and cryptographic verification archives.

Tamper-Proof Manifest Packaging

Packages the Executive IC Memo, CycloneDX 1.6 AI-BOM, claim matrix, and raw traces into a verifiable .zip bundle anchored by RFC 8785 canonical JSON manifest.json. An independent auditor can verify the entire bundle integrity via CLI or API.

compile_diligence_bundle_on_disk() -> verify_bundle_integrity()
